# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and expression.

## Our Standards

### 🤝 Expected Behavior

#### Be Respectful and Inclusive

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards other community members

#### Be Collaborative and Professional

- Work collaboratively with other contributors
- Provide constructive feedback and suggestions
- Be open to learning and teaching others
- Share knowledge generously and help others learn
- Acknowledge good work and contributions from others

#### Be Professional and Ethical

- Maintain professional conduct in all interactions
- Respect confidentiality when appropriate
- Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose them when they arise
- Follow the project's coding standards and best practices
- Test your changes thoroughly before submitting

### 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

#### Harassment

- Use of sexualized language or imagery
- Unwelcome sexual advances or attention
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Stalking or persistent unwanted contact
- Publishing private information without consent

#### Hate Speech

- Discriminatory language targeting protected characteristics
- Hate symbols, imagery, or affiliations
- Promoting hate ideologies or conspiracy theories
- Dismissive comments about diversity and inclusion

#### Disruptive Behavior

- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Trolling or baiting for emotional responses
- Spamming or repeated off-topic content
- Excessive self-promotion without community value
- Off-topic discussions that derail productive conversation

#### Technical Misconduct

- Intentional introduction of malicious code or backdoors
- Sabotage of others' work or contributions
- Plagiarism or claiming others' work as your own
- Exploiting system vulnerabilities without responsible disclosure
- Ignoring security concerns or breaking the build without justification

#### Privacy Violations

- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Recording or sharing private conversations
- Using private information for harassment or manipulation
- Violating reasonable privacy expectations

## 📋 Reporting Guidelines

### How to Report Issues

#### For Immediate Harassment

- **Contact maintainers**: Email wout.vanimpe@student.ehb.be
- **Block and report**: Use platform blocking features
- **Document incidents**: Save screenshots, logs, or messages
- **Include context**: Provide relevant discussion links or timestamps

#### For Code of Conduct Violations

- **Public report**: Use GitHub Issues with `[conduct]` label
- **Private report**: Email maintainers for sensitive matters
- **Provide evidence**: Screenshots, logs, or message content
- **Be specific**: Include usernames, dates, and behavior description

### What to Include in Reports

- **Description**: Clear, factual description of the behavior
- **Context**: Relevant links to discussions, commits, or PRs
- **Impact**: How the behavior affects you or the community
- **Resolution sought**: What outcome you consider appropriate
- **Evidence**: Screenshots, logs, or message content when available

### Confidentiality

- All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality
- Retaliation against reporters will not be tolerated
- False or malicious reports may result in consequences

## ⚖️ Enforcement Process

### Response Timeline

- **24 hours**: Initial acknowledgment of report
- **48-72 hours**: Investigation and response plan
- **1 week**: Resolution and follow-up

### Possible Actions

- **Warning**: For first-time or minor violations
- **Temporary Suspension**: For repeated or moderate violations
- **Permanent Ban**: For severe, repeated, or egregious violations
- **Legal Action**: For illegal behavior or harassment

### Appeals Process

- **Review**: Second maintainer review of the decision
- **Final Decision**: Maintainer team makes final determination
- **Documentation**: All actions are documented for transparency

## 🌟 Positive Environment

### Creating a Welcoming Community

- **Welcome new contributors**: Actively greet and help new members
- **Mentorship**: Offer guidance and support to newcomers
- **Recognize contributions**: Acknowledge and appreciate all valid contributions
- **Constructive feedback**: Focus on improvement, not criticism
- **Celebrate success**: Share positive outcomes and project milestones

### Lead by Example

- **Maintainers**: Model respectful and professional behavior
- **Regular contributors**: Demonstrate inclusive and collaborative conduct
- **Community champions**: Recognize and reward positive community behavior

## 📞 Project-Specific Guidelines

### Development Standards

- Follow all [STANDARDS.md](../STANDARDS.md) conventions

- **GitHub Issues**: For bug reports and feature requests
- **GitHub Discussions**: For general questions and community discussion

### Collaboration Etiquette

- **PR Reviews**: Provide thorough and respectful reviews
- **Issue Triage**: Help prioritize and categorize community issues
